Our mission

We make clothes that outlast the trend cycle.

Field Supply Co. exists because most clothing today is built to be replaced, not repaired. We work with a handful of small mills and workshops, use fabrics that age well instead of wearing out, and stand behind every stitch. If something breaks, we fix it — that's the whole business model.
